**SISCOM**

DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE FINANCE & UNDERWRITING PLATFORM

**Strategic Business Plan, Technology Plan & Marketing Plan**

2026–2030 Strategic Blueprint

_Core thesis: make productive digital infrastructure financeable by combining technology-driven underwriting, embedded collections, alternative data and revenue-based repayment._

Executive Summary
=================

Siscom is designed as a technology and underwriting platform rather than a balance-sheet lender. The platform sits between regulated capital providers and underserved, infrastructure-rich SMEs, turning operating activity, productive assets and transaction flows into a continuously measurable financing profile.

Under the selected Model B structure, Siscom will not originate all loans itself. A sister company, Mkash, regulated by the Central Bank of Kenya, can originate lending facilities, while Early Bird, a sister company operating in the crowdfunding ecosystem and regulated by the Capital Markets Authority, can provide another capital channel where the applicable product and offering structure permits. Siscom will provide underwriting, technology, loan management, collections, monitoring and portfolio intelligence.

The most differentiated product is revenue-share financing for B2C businesses with frequent daily or weekly collections. Siscom integrates into the payment flow so an agreed portion of each customer payment is allocated automatically toward financing, with the balance settling to the business. This creates a payment-controlled repayment mechanism and generates high-frequency operating data for dynamic underwriting.

Strategic positioning: Siscom makes digital infrastructure and recurring business cash flows financeable.

1\. Siscom Business Model
=========================

1.1 The Problem
---------------

*   Many technology-enabled SMEs own or operate productive infrastructure but do not fit conventional collateral and underwriting models.
*   Traditional lenders often rely on historical financial statements, conventional collateral and periodic credit assessments.
*   B2C businesses may have strong daily or weekly collections but fragmented payment channels, making cash-flow underwriting difficult.
*   Financing is often episodic rather than integrated into the operating cycle of the business.
*   Infrastructure performance, customer behaviour and payment data are frequently underused in credit decisions.

1.2 The Siscom Solution
-----------------------

Siscom combines four capabilities into one operating layer:

*   Digital infrastructure and collateral intelligence.
*   Technology-driven underwriting using traditional and alternative data.
*   Embedded payment integration and automatic revenue-share allocation.
*   Continuous portfolio monitoring and dynamic credit management.

4\. Revenue-Share Financing Engine
==================================

Revenue-share is a core differentiator. The repayment mechanism should be designed as a configurable collection waterfall within the Siscom platform.

4.1 Example
-----------

A B2C business receives KES 10,000 from a customer. Under a 12% revenue-share agreement: KES 1,200 is allocated toward financing and KES 8,800 remains available to the business, subject to the final contracted waterfall and any applicable payment charges.

4.2 Payment Waterfall
---------------------

1.  Customer payment initiated.
2.  Siscom-integrated payment layer identifies the transaction and customer/business account.
3.  Revenue-share percentage is calculated.
4.  Financing allocation is posted to the loan ledger.
5.  Residual funds settle to the SME’s designated account or wallet.
6.  The system updates principal outstanding, accrued economics, repayment history and risk indicators.

4.3 Why This Matters
--------------------

*   Repayment capacity is aligned with actual business activity.
*   The business does not face the same rigid instalment burden during a low-revenue period.
*   Siscom receives granular transaction data for continuous underwriting.
*   Collection becomes part of the operating workflow rather than a separate monthly activity.
*   The mechanism can support dynamic credit capacity and early-warning monitoring.

12\. Embedded Distribution & Vendor Strategy
============================================

One of the strongest growth strategies is to move upstream from the borrower to the infrastructure supplier. The equipment vendor becomes a distribution channel for Siscom financing.

The vendor gets paid, the SME receives infrastructure, Siscom receives the technology/underwriting relationship, and the capital provider funds the transaction.

20\. Conclusion
===============

Siscom should be built as a specialist financial infrastructure company—not as a generic digital lender. Its edge is the ability to understand the productive infrastructure behind a business, observe the cash flows generated by that infrastructure, embed itself into the payment process and turn that operating intelligence into better financing decisions.

The Model B structure strengthens this proposition because Siscom can sit above multiple regulated capital sources while retaining the technology, underwriting, collections and portfolio-management relationship. Mkash can provide a regulated lending channel. Early Bird can provide an additional crowdfunding capital channel within the applicable CMA framework. Siscom becomes the common operating and intelligence layer.

Siscom’s north star: Finance the infrastructure. Capture the cash flow. Understand the business. Increase financeability.
